## Definitions

Each entry gives what it is, its purpose, its SAP fit, and a source. Dates appear only where they are needed.

**SAP S/4HANA**
- **What it is:** SAP's ERP suite, built for the SAP HANA in-memory database. Successor to R/3 and SAP ERP.
- **Purpose:** Runs core finance, logistics, and other business processes.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's flagship ERP and the target of ECC migration.
- **Source:** IBM, "What is SAP S/4HANA?" (https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/sap-s4hana, Apr 2026). Third-party; not verified against an SAP primary page.

**SAP ECC (SAP ERP 6.0)**
- **What it is:** SAP's legacy on-premise ERP, delivered as SAP ERP 6.0 with enhancement packages.
- **Purpose:** Runs ERP processes on the pre-S/4HANA architecture.
- **SAP fit:** Being retired in favor of S/4HANA.
- **Source (SAP-authored):** SAP Community, "Maintenance Timelines for SAP ERP 6.0 ('ECC')" (https://community.sap.com/t5/enterprise-resource-planning-blog-posts-by-sap/maintenance-timelines-for-sap-erp-6-0/bc-p/13524576). Mainstream maintenance for EhP 6–8 runs to 31 Dec 2027. Optional extended maintenance runs to 31 Dec 2030. The 2027 and 2030 dates are verified against SAP's own text.
- **Unverified:** The "2033 customer-specific" date is from third-party sources only and is excluded.

**RISE with SAP**
- **What it is:** SAP's bundled transformation offering covering methodology, services, and cloud deployment for moving to S/4HANA.
- **Purpose:** Gets customers from on-premise or legacy systems to cloud S/4HANA.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's main migration route.
- **Source:** SAP product page, "What Is RISE with SAP?" (https://www.sap.com/products/erp/rise/what-is-rise-with-sap.html).
- **Reported, not verified:** The Register reported on Mar 19, 2026 that SAP's RISE migration plan was about €2B short of its targets (https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/03/19/saps-grand-cloud-escape-plan-2b-short-of-the-runway/5221557). I did not check this against SAP filings, so cite it as The Register's reporting only.

**SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP)**
- **What it is:** A platform spanning application development, process automation, integration, data and analytics, and AI.
- **Purpose:** Builds, extends, and integrates SAP and custom applications.
- **SAP fit:** Shared development and integration layer under SAP's AI and data services.
- **Source:** SAP Help Portal, "SAP Business Technology Platform" (https://help.sap.com/docs/btp/sap-business-technology-platform/sap-business-technology-platform).

**SAP Business AI Platform**
- **What it is:** SAP's platform for building, contextualizing, and governing AI agents across the enterprise. SAP's product page lists development, integration, data context, and governance as its components.
- **Purpose:** Deploys, manages, and scales agents with governance.
- **SAP fit:** The AI foundation of SAP's "Autonomous Enterprise" strategy, launched at Sapphire 2026. SAP's Integration Suite page describes it as part of this platform.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/ai-platform.html). The three pillars (Build; Contextualize and Reason; Govern) come from SAP Learning, "Introducing SAP Business AI Platform" (https://learning.sap.com/courses/introducing-sap-business-ai-platform-1).

**SAP AI Core**
- **What it is:** A managed AI service on the Business AI Platform for operating, governing, and scaling AI workloads.
- **Purpose:** Deploys and orchestrates models and AI workflows. Hosts the generative AI hub, which gives governed access to generative AI models.
- **SAP fit:** Back-end AI runtime for developers and partners.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/ai-core.html) and SAP Help Portal, "Generative AI Hub" (https://help.sap.com/docs/sap-ai-core/generative-ai/explainability).

**Joule**
- **What it is:** SAP's generative AI copilot, embedded across SAP applications.
- **Purpose:** Gives a conversational interface to SAP applications and business processes.
- **SAP fit:** The user-facing AI layer. It is embedded in S/4HANA and SuccessFactors, and custom skills and agents are built on it in Joule Studio.
- **Sources:** SAP Community, "Understanding Joule (Step #2)" (https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/understanding-joule-step-2-how-joule-works/ba-p/14062911, Apr 2025); SAP Architecture Center, "Integrating and Extending Joule" (https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ref-arch/464deb), which names S/4HANA and SuccessFactors.

**Joule Work**
- **What it is:** A web-based workspace that connects users to SAP capabilities through conversational AI and orchestrates agents.
- **Purpose:** Moves users from intent to action and automates routine steps.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's workspace for enterprise AI, reworked from the assistant interface at Sapphire 2026.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/joule-work.html) and SAP Help Portal PDF, "Joule Work" (https://help.sap.com/doc/525677ed302148a4b7cc9988321055fb/CLOUD/en-US/dc1706e63f4d443f9da2aeae83a2277e.pdf). The "central workspace" wording is from a release-highlights snippet I did not open, so I cite the product pages instead.

**Joule Studio**
- **What it is:** A managed environment for building and running custom AI agents, apps, and workflows, including a low-code agent builder.
- **Purpose:** Lets business and IT teams create agents that work across SAP and non-SAP systems.
- **SAP fit:** The build tool for custom agents, delivered through SAP Build.
- **Sources:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/joule-studio.html); SAP Community, agent builder general availability (https://community.sap.com/t5/artificial-intelligence-blogs-posts/agent-builder-in-joule-studio-is-now-generally-available-build-your-own/ba-p/14289282, Dec 19, 2025).

**Joule Agents and A2A/MCP**
- **What it is:** Joule Agents are AI agents that run on Joule. A2A (Agent2Agent) and MCP (Model Context Protocol) are the open standards SAP uses so agents and tools can interoperate.
- **Purpose:** Lets agents discover each other, exchange tasks, and access tools without tight coupling.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's interoperability layer for agents across its applications and third-party systems.
- **Sources:** SAP Architecture Center, "A2A and MCP for AI Agent Interoperability at SAP" (https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ref-arch/76ec36); SAP Community, "Joule A2A" (https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/joule-a2a-connect-code-based-agents-into-joule/ba-p/14329279, Feb 2026).

**SAP Autonomous Suite**
- **What it is:** SAP's cloud application portfolio organized by business domain, with AI agents and assistants embedded.
- **Purpose:** Runs business workflows with agents under human oversight.
- **SAP fit:** The application layer of the Autonomous Enterprise strategy.
- **Sources:** SAP Learning course, "Discovering the SAP Autonomous Suite" (https://learning.sap.com/courses/positioning-autonomous-enterprise/discovering-the-sap-autonomous-suite_b9f5cdfc-c867-4798-91ce-94a4177b4192). SAP event page, Sept 22, 2026 webinar (https://www.sap.com/events/2026-09-22-online-powering-the-autonomous-enterprise-with-ai-agents-and-cloud-erp-private-americas.html), which gives the counts: five domains, more than 200 agents, over 50 Joule assistants. Those counts are SAP's own claims.

**SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC)**
- **What it is:** A fully managed SaaS solution that unifies and governs SAP and third-party data through a business data fabric.
- **Purpose:** Provides a governed, AI-ready data foundation.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's unified data platform. Per SAP's product page, it combines SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud, and SAP Business Warehouse, plus managed SAP Databricks and intelligent applications.
- **Sources:** SAP product page, "What is SAP Business Data Cloud?" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/what-is-sap-business-data-cloud.html); SAP product page, "SAP Databricks in Business Data Cloud" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/databricks.html), which gives the component list.

**SAP Datasphere**
- **What it is:** SAP's data management and modeling layer, described by SAP as the "knowledge core" of BDC. It unifies semantics, data products, and modeling across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.
- **Purpose:** Converges SAP and third-party data into one managed cloud environment for analytics and governed access.
- **SAP fit:** The semantic and modeling core within BDC.
- **Sources:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/datasphere.html); SAP Help Portal, "Getting Started with SAP Datasphere" (https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_DATASPHERE/d4f3c5a0bb074d09ae9b42b2b9bd7a08/2377bb6efedb42d9a6796f0d7d68f1a4.html).

**SAP Databricks**
- **What it is:** An SAP-managed version of the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, embedded natively in BDC.
- **Purpose:** Provides AI/ML, data science, and data engineering on SAP business data, using zero-copy Delta Sharing.
- **SAP fit:** The AI and ML engine within BDC.
- **Source:** SAP product page, "What is SAP Databricks?" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/what-is-sap-databricks.html).

**SAP HANA Cloud**
- **What it is:** A multi-model cloud database that processes relational, graph, vector, spatial, JSON, and time-series data in one engine.
- **Purpose:** Stores and processes enterprise data in real time, including for AI workloads.
- **SAP fit:** The database foundation for S/4HANA-era data and AI. It is related to BDC through SAP's product page for HANA Cloud in BDC.
- **Sources:** SAP product page, "SAP HANA Cloud in Business Data Cloud" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/hana.html); SAP Help Portal, "SAP HANA Cloud" (https://help.sap.com/docs/r/p/HANA_CLOUD_DATABASE).

**SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)**
- **What it is:** An end-to-end cloud solution combining business intelligence, augmented and predictive analytics, and enterprise planning.
- **Purpose:** Reporting, analysis, and planning in one system.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's analytics and planning application, which is also a component of BDC.
- **Source:** SAP Help Portal, "What Is SAP Analytics Cloud?" (https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_ANALYTICS_CLOUD/18850a0e13944f53aa8a8b7c094ea29e/449cc17118304d73b2d50d562eff50a5.html).

**SAP Business Warehouse (BW / BW/4HANA)**
- **What it is:** SAP's data warehouse. BW/4HANA is the next-generation version, optimized for SAP HANA.
- **Purpose:** Builds and manages an enterprise data warehouse with a simplified architecture.
- **SAP fit:** The warehouse layer that BDC includes, alongside Datasphere.
- **Sources:** SAP Help Portal, "SAP BW/4HANA" (https://help.sap.com/docs/r/p/SAP_BW4HANA); SAP product page for BDC (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/databricks.html), which lists Business Warehouse as a component.

**SAP Integration Suite**

- **SAP fit:** Connectivity layer for the Business AI Platform and BTP.

## Definitions

Each entry gives what it is, its purpose, its SAP fit, and a source. Dates appear only where they are needed.

**SAP S/4HANA**
- **What it is:** SAP's ERP suite, built for the SAP HANA in-memory database. Successor to R/3 and SAP ERP.
- **Purpose:** Runs core finance, logistics, and other business processes.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's flagship ERP and the target of ECC migration.
- **Source:** IBM, "What is SAP S/4HANA?" (https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/sap-s4hana, Apr 2026). Third-party; not verified against an SAP primary page.

**SAP ECC (SAP ERP 6.0)**
- **What it is:** SAP's legacy on-premise ERP, delivered as SAP ERP 6.0 with enhancement packages.
- **Purpose:** Runs ERP processes on the pre-S/4HANA architecture.
- **SAP fit:** Being retired in favor of S/4HANA.
- **Source (SAP-authored):** SAP Community, "Maintenance Timelines for SAP ERP 6.0 ('ECC')" (https://community.sap.com/t5/enterprise-resource-planning-blog-posts-by-sap/maintenance-timelines-for-sap-erp-6-0/bc-p/13524576). Mainstream maintenance for EhP 6–8 runs to 31 Dec 2027. Optional extended maintenance runs to 31 Dec 2030. The 2027 and 2030 dates are verified against SAP's own text.
- **Unverified:** The "2033 customer-specific" date is from third-party sources only and is excluded.

**RISE with SAP**
- **What it is:** SAP's bundled transformation offering covering methodology, services, and cloud deployment for moving to S/4HANA.
- **Purpose:** Gets customers from on-premise or legacy systems to cloud S/4HANA.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's main migration route.
- **Source:** SAP product page, "What Is RISE with SAP?" (https://www.sap.com/products/erp/rise/what-is-rise-with-sap.html).
- **Reported, not verified:** The Register reported on Mar 19, 2026 that SAP's RISE migration plan was about €2B short of its targets (https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/03/19/saps-grand-cloud-escape-plan-2b-short-of-the-runway/5221557). I did not check this against SAP filings, so cite it as The Register's reporting only.

**SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP)**
- **What it is:** A platform spanning application development, process automation, integration, data and analytics, and AI.
- **Purpose:** Builds, extends, and integrates SAP and custom applications.
- **SAP fit:** Shared development and integration layer under SAP's AI and data services.
- **Source:** SAP Help Portal, "SAP Business Technology Platform" (https://help.sap.com/docs/btp/sap-business-technology-platform/sap-business-technology-platform).

**SAP Business AI Platform**
- **What it is:** SAP's platform for building, contextualizing, and governing AI agents across the enterprise. SAP's product page lists development, integration, data context, and governance as its components.
- **Purpose:** Deploys, manages, and scales agents with governance.
- **SAP fit:** The AI foundation of SAP's "Autonomous Enterprise" strategy, launched at Sapphire 2026. SAP's Integration Suite page describes it as part of this platform.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/ai-platform.html). The three pillars (Build; Contextualize and Reason; Govern) come from SAP Learning, "Introducing SAP Business AI Platform" (https://learning.sap.com/courses/introducing-sap-business-ai-platform-1).

**SAP AI Core**
- **What it is:** A managed AI service on the Business AI Platform for operating, governing, and scaling AI workloads.
- **Purpose:** Deploys and orchestrates models and AI workflows. Hosts the generative AI hub, which gives governed access to generative AI models.
- **SAP fit:** Back-end AI runtime for developers and partners.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/ai-core.html) and SAP Help Portal, "Generative AI Hub" (https://help.sap.com/docs/sap-ai-core/generative-ai/explainability).

**Joule**
- **What it is:** SAP's generative AI copilot, embedded across SAP applications.
- **Purpose:** Gives a conversational interface to SAP applications and business processes.
- **SAP fit:** The user-facing AI layer. It is embedded in S/4HANA and SuccessFactors, and custom skills and agents are built on it in Joule Studio.
- **Sources:** SAP Community, "Understanding Joule (Step #2)" (https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/understanding-joule-step-2-how-joule-works/ba-p/14062911, Apr 2025); SAP Architecture Center, "Integrating and Extending Joule" (https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ref-arch/464deb), which names S/4HANA and SuccessFactors.

**Joule Work**
- **What it is:** A web-based workspace that connects users to SAP capabilities through conversational AI and orchestrates agents.
- **Purpose:** Moves users from intent to action and automates routine steps.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's workspace for enterprise AI, reworked from the assistant interface at Sapphire 2026.
- **Source:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/joule-work.html) and SAP Help Portal PDF, "Joule Work" (https://help.sap.com/doc/525677ed302148a4b7cc9988321055fb/CLOUD/en-US/dc1706e63f4d443f9da2aeae83a2277e.pdf). The "central workspace" wording is from a release-highlights snippet I did not open, so I cite the product pages instead.

**Joule Studio**
- **What it is:** A managed environment for building and running custom AI agents, apps, and workflows, including a low-code agent builder.
- **Purpose:** Lets business and IT teams create agents that work across SAP and non-SAP systems.
- **SAP fit:** The build tool for custom agents, delivered through SAP Build.
- **Sources:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/artificial-intelligence/joule-studio.html); SAP Community, agent builder general availability (https://community.sap.com/t5/artificial-intelligence-blogs-posts/agent-builder-in-joule-studio-is-now-generally-available-build-your-own/ba-p/14289282, Dec 19, 2025).

**Joule Agents and A2A/MCP**
- **What it is:** Joule Agents are AI agents that run on Joule. A2A (Agent2Agent) and MCP (Model Context Protocol) are the open standards SAP uses so agents and tools can interoperate.
- **Purpose:** Lets agents discover each other, exchange tasks, and access tools without tight coupling.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's interoperability layer for agents across its applications and third-party systems.
- **Sources:** SAP Architecture Center, "A2A and MCP for AI Agent Interoperability at SAP" (https://architecture.learning.sap.com/docs/ref-arch/76ec36); SAP Community, "Joule A2A" (https://community.sap.com/t5/technology-blog-posts-by-sap/joule-a2a-connect-code-based-agents-into-joule/ba-p/14329279, Feb 2026).

**SAP Autonomous Suite**
- **What it is:** SAP's cloud application portfolio organized by business domain, with AI agents and assistants embedded.
- **Purpose:** Runs business workflows with agents under human oversight.
- **SAP fit:** The application layer of the Autonomous Enterprise strategy.
- **Sources:** SAP Learning course, "Discovering the SAP Autonomous Suite" (https://learning.sap.com/courses/positioning-autonomous-enterprise/discovering-the-sap-autonomous-suite_b9f5cdfc-c867-4798-91ce-94a4177b4192). - **Sources:** SAP product page, "What is SAP Business Data Cloud?" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/what-is-sap-business-data-cloud.html); SAP product page, "SAP Databricks in Business Data Cloud" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/databricks.html), which gives the component list.

**SAP Datasphere**
- **What it is:** SAP's data management and modeling layer, described by SAP as the "knowledge core" of BDC. It unifies semantics, data products, and modeling across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.
- **Purpose:** Converges SAP and third-party data into one managed cloud environment for analytics and governed access.
- **SAP fit:** The semantic and modeling core within BDC.
- **Sources:** SAP product page (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/datasphere.html); SAP Help Portal, "Getting Started with SAP Datasphere" (https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_DATASPHERE/d4f3c5a0bb074d09ae9b42b2b9bd7a08/2377bb6efedb42d9a6796f0d7d68f1a4.html).

**SAP Databricks**
- **What it is:** An SAP-managed version of the Databricks Data Intelligence Platform, embedded natively in BDC.
- **Purpose:** Provides AI/ML, data science, and data engineering on SAP business data, using zero-copy Delta Sharing.
- **SAP fit:** The AI and ML engine within BDC.
- **Source:** SAP product page, "What is SAP Databricks?" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/what-is-sap-databricks.html).

**SAP HANA Cloud**
- **What it is:** A multi-model cloud database that processes relational, graph, vector, spatial, JSON, and time-series data in one engine.
- **Purpose:** Stores and processes enterprise data in real time, including for AI workloads.
- **SAP fit:** The database foundation for S/4HANA-era data and AI. It is related to BDC through SAP's product page for HANA Cloud in BDC.
- **Sources:** SAP product page, "SAP HANA Cloud in Business Data Cloud" (https://www.sap.com/products/data-cloud/hana.html); SAP Help Portal, "SAP HANA Cloud" (https://help.sap.com/docs/r/p/HANA_CLOUD_DATABASE).

**SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)**

- **Purpose:** Reporting, analysis, and planning in one system.
- **SAP fit:** SAP's analytics and planning application, which is also a component of BDC.
- **Source:** SAP Help Portal, "What Is SAP Analytics Cloud?" (https://help.sap.com/docs/SAP_ANALYTICS_CLOUD/18850a0e13944f53aa8a8b7c094ea29e/449cc17118304d73b2d50d562eff50a5.html).

**SAP Business Warehouse (BW / BW/4HANA)**

# SAP Technology News: What's Happening, What Each Technology Is, and How Businesses Use It

As of the latest items I could find (late October 2026), SAP's news is dominated by one strategy: the **"Autonomous Enterprise,"** in which AI agents work across SAP business applications. Underneath that sit a legacy migration problem (the ECC end-of-support deadline) and a consolidation of SAP's data products. Most product news comes from SAP's own channels, so I've flagged which claims are independent and which are vendor-supplied.

## Where to Follow SAP News

No source here has been independently ranked for reputation, so treat this as a practical guide to what each source is good for:

- **The Register** is the most useful independent voice. Its 2026 coverage includes critical reporting on SAP's migration shortfall, licensing, and ECC pressure. Its specific claims are reported, not confirmed, so check them before citing.
- **ComputerWeekly and TechTarget SearchSAP** provide mainstream enterprise IT coverage with dated 2026 stories.
- **SAP News Center (news.sap.com)** is the authoritative source for product facts and announcements, but it is vendor-run. Use it for what SAP says, not for analysis.
- **SAP Community** has official announcements and practitioner how-tos. It mixes SAP-authored and member-written posts.
- **ASUG, UKISUG, and DSAG** are SAP user groups. Their joint research on AI adoption is useful for customer sentiment.
- **Diginomica and SAPinsider** cover SAP frequently, but both carry sponsored content, so check labels.

## The Big Stories

**1. The Autonomous Enterprise (Sapphire, May 2026; SAP Connect, October 2026).** SAP launched the SAP Business AI Platform as the foundation for AI agents. Joule became the conversational layer. Joule Work, the workspace for enterprise AI, began its customer rollout on October 6, 2026, with availability expected to run into early 2027. The figures SAP cites (over 50 Joule assistants, more than 200 agents) are its own claims.

**2. Data consolidation (SAP Business Data Cloud, or BDC).** BDC combines SAP Datasphere, SAP Analytics Cloud, and Business Warehouse with managed SAP Databricks. October 2026 announcements add lakehouse and data-fabric capabilities meant to let agents act with more autonomy.

**3. The ECC deadline and migration struggle.** Mainstream maintenance for SAP ECC ends December 31, 2027, with extended maintenance through 2030 (SAP's own maintenance post). The Register reported in March 2026 that SAP's RISE migration plan was about €2B short of target, and that only 39% of ECC customers had begun migrating by the end of 2024. Those figures are reported, not verified against SAP filings. Consultancies that sell migration services also emphasize the urgency, so weigh their advice accordingly.

**4. TechEd 2026 (Berlin, October 27–29).** The developer focus is agentic AI on BTP, BDC, and Joule Studio.

## What Each Technology Is, With an Example

Evidence labels: **[SAP]** SAP-published; **[Partner]** partner or cloud-provider case study; **[Press]** journalism; **[Practitioner]** community post; **[Customer-supplied]** metrics not independently checked; **[Hypothetical]** illustrative only, with no named customer found.

### Core ERP

**SAP S/4HANA** is SAP's flagship ERP suite, built on the SAP HANA in-memory database. It runs finance, logistics, and other core processes.
*Example [SAP]:* DACHSER, a German logistics company, moved its HR system to S/4HANA Cloud through RISE with SAP in October 2026. I read only the summary.

**SAP ECC (ERP 6.0)** is the legacy on-premises ERP that S/4HANA is replacing. Its support is ending, which is why migration decisions are urgent.
*Example [Press, reported]:* Kingfisher, a UK retailer, reportedly chose third-party support from Rimini Street and moved its ECC platform to Google Cloud rather than follow SAP's recommended S/4HANA path. The Register reported this in November 2025. I did not confirm it with Kingfisher.

**RISE with SAP** is SAP's bundled offering of methodology, services, and cloud deployment for moving to S/4HANA. It is SAP's main migration route.
*Example [Partner]:* Lion moved a self-managed S/4HANA system to RISE on AWS, transferring 4 TB of data and keeping about 50 integrations running. This is an AWS case study.

### Platform and AI

**SAP Business Technology Platform (BTP)** is the shared platform for building, extending, and integrating SAP and custom applications. It is the development layer beneath SAP's AI and data services.
*Example [Customer-supplied]:* FLO built a machine-learning app on BTP, using HANA Cloud and Analytics Cloud, to predict defective returns. It reports a 50% reduction in inspection workload. The metrics come from the customer.

**SAP Business AI Platform** is the platform for building, contextualizing, and governing AI agents. It is the foundation of the Autonomous Enterprise strategy.
*Example [SAP, headline only]:* Martur Fompak uses SAP Business AI to automate material replenishment in its manufacturing operations. I saw only the summary.

**SAP AI Core** is a managed service for running and governing AI workloads. It hosts the generative AI hub, which gives controlled access to foundation models.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* A manufacturer routes a foundation model through the generative AI hub to classify supplier invoices, with model access governed centrally. No named customer was found.

**Joule** is SAP's generative AI copilot, embedded across SAP applications such as S/4HANA and SuccessFactors. It gives users a conversational interface to business processes.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* A procurement lead asks Joule which purchase requisitions are stuck, and gets links to the approval steps in S/4HANA. No named customer was confirmed for Joule itself.

**Joule Work** is a web-based workspace that connects users to SAP capabilities and orchestrates agents. It moves a user from intent to action.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* A plant supervisor asks Joule Work to find overdue maintenance orders and assign them to available technicians. The rollout is confirmed, but no customer case is.

**Joule Studio** is a managed environment for building custom agents, with a low-code builder that works across SAP and non-SAP systems.
*Example [Practitioner]:* A developer built an agent in Joule Studio that reads and updates S/4HANA sales orders through OData APIs. Joule could explain the process but could not execute it on its own, so the custom agent was needed.

**Joule Agents, A2A, and MCP.** Joule Agents are task-specific agents that run on Joule. A2A (Agent2Agent) and MCP (Model Context Protocol) are open standards that let agents discover tools and each other across vendors. SAP supports them, but they are not SAP products.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* A freight carrier's quote-generating agent, built outside SAP, is called by a Joule Agent during order entry. No live deployment was found.

**SAP Autonomous Suite** is SAP's cloud application portfolio, organized by business domain, with agents embedded in each domain. Agents run workflows under human oversight.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* In procurement, an agent matches purchase orders, goods receipts, and invoices, and routes mismatches to a buyer. SAP lists the domain and agent counts, but I found no customer outcome.

### Data and Analytics

**SAP Business Data Cloud (BDC)** is a fully managed SaaS product that unifies and governs SAP and third-party data for AI. It is SAP's main data platform.
*Example [Partner, vendor-presented]:* Adidas connected SAP applications to its Databricks lakehouse through BDC using zero-copy sharing. It reports that its IBP cycle times fell from hours to minutes. The case was co-presented by Databricks.

**SAP Datasphere** is the data modeling and semantic layer at the core of BDC. It unifies data products and business meaning across environments.
*Example [Partner, outcome not read]:* PwC uses Datasphere to consolidate data from offices in more than 150 countries.

**SAP Databricks** is an SAP-managed version of the Databricks platform, embedded in BDC. It handles AI, machine learning, and data engineering on SAP data.
*Example:* The same Adidas case above. It accesses BDC data products from a Databricks workspace without copying them.

**SAP HANA Cloud** is a multi-model cloud database that handles relational, graph, vector, spatial, JSON, and time-series data in one engine. It is the database foundation for SAP's data and AI work.
*Example [Hypothetical]:* A retailer runs demand forecasting inside HANA Cloud on years of point-of-sale data, without moving the data out.

**SAP Analytics Cloud (SAC)** combines business intelligence, predictive analytics, and planning in one cloud product.
*Example [Partner, outcome not read]:* Embraer uses SAC for planning, which the customer story describes as improving budget control.

**SAP Business Warehouse (BW/4HANA)** is SAP's data warehouse, optimized for HANA. It is part of BDC's architecture.
*Example [Partner]:* An anonymized aviation company migrated about 5 TB of SAP BW on HANA to BW/4HANA to simplify its data models. The source states the objectives, not confirmed results.

**SAP Integration Suite** is SAP's integration platform as a service. It connects applications, data, and AI agents across SAP and non-SAP systems.
*Example [Customer-supplied]:* National Bank of Canada reportedly cut B2B partner integration from about two months to one week, saving about $350,000 a year. The figures come from the bank and were presented in SAP's Innovation Awards material.
